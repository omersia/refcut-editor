What this free prototype can and cannot do

✓ Local file selection · reference duration and aspect ratio · rough visual-change sampling · segment timing plan · browser-based preview/export where supported.

⚠️ It cannot guarantee exact style cloning. Export is a silent video (no source audio), uses browser-supported WebM encoding, and may fail on large files or browsers without MediaRecorder support. Keep the screen awake during export.

Privacy: selected videos are processed in this page and are not intentionally uploaded to a server. Refreshing the page clears the current session.
